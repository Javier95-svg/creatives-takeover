# Community example photos

Profile photos for the invented people in the Community examples (Rooms posts and Launchpad makers).

**Rules**
- Only AI-generated faces of people who do not exist, from a source whose licence allows commercial use (for example Generated Photos). Never a photo of a real person.
- Square JPG, at least 256×256, named exactly `<handle>.jpg`.
- After adding a file, add its handle to `EXAMPLE_PHOTO_HANDLES` in `src/lib/communityExamples.ts`. Anyone not listed keeps the drawn avatar.
- The examples keep their "Example" tag and the note that the people are invented.
